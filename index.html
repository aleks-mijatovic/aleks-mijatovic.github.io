Aleksandar Mijatović

Professor of Probability, University of Warwick

My research centres on probability, from the structure and limiting behaviour of stochastic processes to the development and analysis of numerical methods. My interests include Monte Carlo sampling, the mathematical foundations of machine learning, and mathematical finance, with connections to analysis and geometry.
